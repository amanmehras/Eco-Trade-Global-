# Shipment Tracking Features - EcoTrade Global

## New Features Added

Your B2B marketplace now includes comprehensive shipment tracking for international scrap material trading!

## For Shippers (Sellers)

### Shipment Information Fields:
- Sales Order Number
- Shipment Date and Estimated Arrival
- Port of Loading and Port of Arrival
- Shipping Line and Container Number
- Shipment Status (Not Shipped, In Transit, Arrived, Delivered)
- Shipment Notes

### Upload Shipment Documents:
- Commercial Invoice
- Packing List
- Bill of Lading
- Certificate of Origin
- Inspection Report
- Other Documents

## For Buyers

### Track Your Shipment:
- View real-time shipment status
- See port information and shipping details
- Download all shipping documents
- Monitor estimated arrival dates

## How to Access:
- Shippers: Orders Page → "Manage Shipment" button
- Buyers: Orders Page → "View" → See "Shipment Tracking" section
